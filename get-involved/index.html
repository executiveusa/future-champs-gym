Future Champs documents young and beginning fighters and the people supporting them. Story suggestions and partnership inquiries will use a reviewed contact process.

Story suggestions

We are preparing a safe submission channel. It is not open yet. Do not send personal information about a young person, private documents, or footage through an unverified contact route.

Partnerships

Coaches, gyms, and community programs.

We intend to hear from people who support beginning fighters. A verified contact destination will be published when it is ready.

Rights and youth safety

Permission before publication.

No youth footage or profile will be published without the applicable permissions and editorial review. Please do not submit personal or sensitive youth information through this public website.
