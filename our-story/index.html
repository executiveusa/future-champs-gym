03 / The origin

A FATHER. A SON. THE GREATEST.

Future Champs began with Glen Singleton Jr.’s wish to honor his father and Muhammad Ali.

The history

Glen Singleton and Muhammad Ali

Reporting from Louisiana documented Glen Singleton’s long-standing admiration for Muhammad Ali, including the memorabilia he collected and his history following Ali’s career.

Future Champs is conceived as Glen Singleton Jr.’s dedication to his father and to Ali. The family’s fuller account of that connection, photographs and personal memories will be published only with Glen Jr.’s review and approval.

About the founder ↗

Editorial boundaries

This is an independent documentary project, not an official Muhammad Ali organization. Archival images, personal correspondence and family stories are held for permission and factual review.

Background reporting: WDSU (2016) and Herald-Guide (2010). Family account pending first-person confirmation.
