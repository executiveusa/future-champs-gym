# Future Champs Brand Kit Flipbook Specification

## Purpose

This document defines the repeatable process for creating and approving the Future Champs identity before website production begins. The brand kit will be presented as an interactive flipbook and will become the source of truth for the website, flyers, social media, food fundraiser, sponsorship materials, uniforms, signage, and future program materials.

## Project mode

- Mode: Greenfield
- Immediate outcome: Approve a coherent Future Champs identity and launch-ready asset system
- Primary brand: Future Champs
- Descriptor: Youth Boxing & Mentorship
- Core line: Building Champions In and Out of the Ring
- Fundraiser: A Taste of Nawlins
- Primary working color direction: red, white, black, with supporting neutral tones
- Current stage: Identity exploration and logo construction

## Non-negotiable design laws

1. The primary logo must be created as true vector paths.
2. No embedded raster artwork inside SVG files.
3. No gradients, bevels, chrome, drop shadows, faux 3D, glow, or texture in the master logo.
4. The logo must work in one color before color is applied.
5. The mark must remain recognizable at favicon size and on embroidery.
6. The identity must not resemble an esports logo, fight-night flyer, clip-art badge, or generic AI sports logo.
7. The primary logo, monogram, and heritage seal must be separate assets with different jobs.
8. The food fundraiser must have a related but distinct identity.
9. Cannabis imagery or language must never be merged into youth-facing brand assets.
10. No website or campaign design proceeds until the identity system is approved.

## Recommended color system

The founder-preferred red-and-white direction should remain central, but red and white alone can become generic. The system therefore uses black and warm neutral tones to create authority, restraint, and versatility.

### Primary colors

- Future Red: #B31F2B
- Championship Black: #111111
- Pure White: #FFFFFF

### Supporting colors

- Warm Bone: #F3EEE7
- Steel Gray: #6D7378
- Deep Red: #75151D

### Usage ratio

- 45% white or warm bone
- 30% black
- 20% Future Red
- 5% supporting gray or deep red

Red should identify actions, highlights, uniforms, and key brand moments. It should not fill every surface.

## Recraft master prompt — Future Champs primary wordmark

Create a premium, fully editable vector identity for a youth boxing and mentorship organization named “FUTURE CHAMPS.”

BRAND POSITIONING:
A respected Pacific Northwest youth institution rooted in historic boxing culture, family legacy, discipline, mentorship, dignity, and community service.

PRIMARY TEXT:
FUTURE CHAMPS

SECONDARY DESCRIPTOR:
YOUTH BOXING & MENTORSHIP

TAGLINE:
BUILDING CHAMPIONS IN AND OUT OF THE RING

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Create a disciplined, timeless sports-institution identity rather than a fight-night poster, esports logo, mascot logo, video-game badge, or generic AI sports emblem.

The primary mark must prioritize a powerful custom wordmark. Incorporate only one restrained boxing reference, such as two simplified horizontal boxing-ring ropes, a subtle squared-ring corner, or an abstract championship stance.

Do not use a detailed boxer silhouette in the primary logo. Do not use fists, flames, wings, crowns, lightning, aggressive faces, photorealistic gloves, excessive stars, gradients, chrome, bevels, shadows, 3D effects, distressed textures, or raster effects.

VISUAL CHARACTER:
- dignified
- disciplined
- historic
- community-centered
- strong without appearing violent
- suitable for children, parents, schools, donors, coaches, and sponsors
- Pacific Northwest institutional character
- classic boxing heritage without copying any existing boxing organization

COLOR DIRECTION FOR LATER APPLICATION:
- Future Red #B31F2B
- Championship Black #111111
- Pure White #FFFFFF
- Warm Bone #F3EEE7

VECTOR REQUIREMENTS:
- true editable SVG paths
- clean closed paths
- minimal anchor points
- consistent curves
- no embedded raster images
- no clipping-mask dependency
- no gradients in the master version
- no unnecessary micro-details
- readable at 24 pixels
- suitable for one-color printing
- suitable for embroidery
- suitable for screen printing
- suitable for vinyl cutting
- suitable for signage
- suitable for favicon reduction

DELIVERABLE:
Present four distinct black-and-white primary wordmark concepts on a plain neutral background. Do not create mockups. Do not place the logo on shirts, walls, gloves, merchandise, or photographs. Do not invent additional wording. Spell all text exactly.

## Vector acceptance test

A logo passes only when:

- the SVG opens as editable paths in a vector editor;
- there are no linked or embedded raster images;
- all text intended for final delivery is converted to outlines in the production master while an editable-text working file is retained;
- the logo remains legible at 24 pixels;
- the one-color version is clear;
- the reversed white version is clear;
- the logo survives grayscale printing;
- no element depends on shadow, glow, texture, or gradient;
- anchor points are controlled and not unnecessarily dense;
- the mark can be embroidered without losing its identity;
- the mark is not confusingly similar to a known sports or boxing logo.

## Flipbook structure

### Page 1 — Cover

Future Champs
Youth Boxing & Mentorship
Building Champions In and Out of the Ring

Purpose: Establish the identity with one confident visual statement.

### Page 2 — The opportunity

Explain the founder’s vision, current reality, and why the identity is being simplified.

Purpose: Show that the brand system creates focus rather than erasing the larger vision.

### Page 3 — Brand foundation

- mission
- audience
- promise
- values
- personality

Purpose: Connect visual choices to organizational purpose.

### Page 4 — Brand architecture

Show the relationship between Future Champs, The First Five, A Taste of Nawlins, TOJO Legacy Project, and the future facility.

Purpose: Prevent every idea from becoming a separate unrelated brand.

### Page 5 — Existing identity audit

Show the strongest and weakest qualities of the current raster graphics without ridiculing the founder’s work.

Evaluate:
- recognizability
- legibility
- scalability
- consistency
- reproduction
- emotional tone

Purpose: Explain why a system is needed.

### Page 6 — Logo concept rationale

Present approved wordmark directions and explain what each communicates.

Purpose: Decision support, not decoration.

### Page 7 — Primary logo

Show the final primary wordmark with clear-space and minimum-size guidance.

### Page 8 — FC monogram

Show favicon, profile image, embroidery, and compact-use logic.

### Page 9 — Heritage seal

Explain when this richer emblem is appropriate and when it is not.

### Page 10 — Color system

Show primary, supporting, contrast, and accessibility combinations.

### Page 11 — Typography

Show real Future Champs copy using the approved hierarchy.

### Page 12 — Photography direction

Define documentary, human, disciplined imagery. Reject fake sweat, over-processed HDR, generic stock boxing, and AI-generated children.

### Page 13 — Graphic language

Show ring-line geometry, borders, section markers, numbers, and restrained athletic cues.

### Page 14 — Social system

Show profile image, post system, story cover, event post, founder story, fundraiser update, and impact post.

### Page 15 — Flyer system

Show one message, one action, one QR code, verified information, and consistent hierarchy.

### Page 16 — A Taste of Nawlins

Show the relationship and separation between the food fundraiser and youth brand.

### Page 17 — Digital application

Show the proposed website header, hero, call to action, mobile behavior, and form style.

### Page 18 — Merchandise and signage

Only after logo approval, demonstrate one-color and limited-color applications.

### Page 19 — Do and do not

Define misuse examples:
- stretching
- adding shadows
- changing colors
- combining marks
- using cannabis imagery
- putting long text inside the primary logo

### Page 20 — Launch kit

List final files, templates, ownership, approval date, and source-of-truth repository location.

## Interactive flipbook behavior

The brand kit should be built as a responsive editorial website rather than a novelty page-turn animation.

Recommended behavior:

- full-viewport chapters
- restrained scroll transitions
- sticky chapter navigation
- keyboard-accessible next and previous controls
- progress indicator
- desktop and mobile layouts
- optional presentation mode
- downloadable PDF later
- reduced-motion support
- no heavy 3D page curl
- no interaction that slows reading

## Awwwards review gate

The flipbook must be scored before presentation.

### Design — 40 points

- typography hierarchy
- composition and grid
- asset quality
- brand consistency
- color discipline
- finish

### Usability — 30 points

- immediate clarity
- navigation
- mobile experience
- accessibility
- readable pacing
- clear decisions

### Creativity — 20 points

- distinctive brand expression
- original editorial composition
- meaningful transitions
- coherent storytelling

### Content — 10 points

- accuracy
- founder voice
- useful rationale
- no unsupported claims

Release threshold:

- minimum overall score: 8.5/10
- no category below 7.5/10
- accessibility, mobile, and navigation cannot fail

## Repeatable studio process

This sequence becomes the standard for future client brand projects:

1. Intake and founder interview
2. Existing-asset inventory
3. Current-state brand audit
4. Audience and positioning lock
5. Brand architecture
6. Creative territory exploration
7. Black-and-white logo concepts
8. Vector verification
9. Color system
10. Typography system
11. Photography and graphic language
12. Real-application tests
13. Interactive brand-kit flipbook
14. Independent review
15. Client decision and approval
16. Final asset packaging
17. Website wireframe
18. Production implementation
19. Verification and launch
20. Governance and change control

## Definition of done for this phase

This phase is complete only when:

- the primary logo is approved in black and white;
- the FC monogram is approved;
- the selected marks pass the vector acceptance test;
- the red, white, black, and neutral palette is approved;
- the typography specimen is approved;
- the brand architecture is approved;
- the flipbook outline and real copy are approved;
- no unverified claims appear;
- the source files and exports are stored in the repository or approved asset system;
- a separate reviewer scores the kit at 8.5/10 or better.
