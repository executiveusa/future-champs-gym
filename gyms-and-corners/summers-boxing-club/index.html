SUMMERS BOXING CLUB

A career in the ring. A second calling in the gym.

Coach / Arlington, Washington

Bret Summers

Bret Summers began boxing at age eight, became a national amateur champion in 1976, and finished his professional career with a 28–3–1 record, according to the Snohomish County Sports Commission. He now coaches amateur and professional boxers in Arlington.

Summers Boxing Club also describes a non-contact boxing fitness program for people living with Parkinson’s disease. Contact the gym for current programs and availability; this profile makes no medical-outcome claims.
