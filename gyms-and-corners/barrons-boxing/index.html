BARRON'S BOXING

A former heavyweight at the center of the room.

Founder and coach / Burlington, Washington

Sylvester “Doc” Barron

Sylvester “Doc” Barron is a U.S. Marine Corps veteran and former professional heavyweight boxer. After his fighting career, he founded Barron’s Boxing in Burlington, where he coaches beginners and experienced athletes.

The gym identifies Michelle Damrill as a co-owner and lists junior and adult instruction, private training and open-gym options. Visitors should confirm current details directly with Barron’s.
