01 / Arlington, WA

Summers Boxing Club

Coach Bret Summers trains amateur and professional boxers in Arlington. His club also offers a non-contact boxing fitness program for people living with Parkinson's disease.

Coach: Bret Summers

02 / Burlington, WA

Barron's Boxing

A Burlington gym founded by former professional heavyweight Sylvester “Doc” Barron. Training includes adult and junior classes, open-gym sessions, and private coaching.

Owner and coach: Sylvester “Doc” Barron
