01 / Gym profile · Seattle, Washington

NOMAD BOXING CLUB

The coach you had becomes the coach you are.

Founder and coach / Seattle, Washington

Manuel “Manny” Dunham

Manuel “Manny” Dunham founded Nomad Boxing Club in 2019. According to Nomad’s own biography, he spent 27 years boxing and brings that experience into coaching people with a wide range of fitness and competition goals.

Nomad describes a coaching community shaped by mentorship. The club’s public biographies include Coaches Gato and Isaiah, both of whom describe the influence of a mentor known as Coach Bumblebee. Training details and locations should be confirmed with Nomad.
