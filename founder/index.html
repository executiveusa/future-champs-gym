04 / The founder

GLEN SINGLETON JR.

Future Champs is Glen Jr.’s project, dedicated to his father and Muhammad Ali.

The project

Why Future Champs

The project will tell the stories of independent boxing gyms, their owners, their coaches and the fighters who begin there. Its editorial approach puts the people doing the work at the center of each story.

Glen Jr.’s personal biography and firsthand account of his father will appear here after he approves the language and any supporting family archive material.
