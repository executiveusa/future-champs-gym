## Anchor selection rule

The first public anchor should be the cleanest approved file that answers one of these questions in the subject's own words:

- Why did you start boxing?
- What are you learning right now?
- Who is in your corner?
- What does the gym give you outside the ring?

If no clean master exists, MACS should request or create an approved clean export from the original source. Do not crop out a watermark or remove attribution without permission.
