The First Five

Start small. Prove the program. Grow responsibly.

The initial concept is a controlled five-participant youth boxing and mentorship pilot built around boxing fundamentals, fitness, discipline, mentorship, parent involvement, and documented progress.

Five participants

A deliberately limited first cohort.

Proposed eight weeks

Final schedule pending facility and family approval.

Evidence before expansion

Attendance, consent, progress notes, parent feedback, and a closing report.

Founder story

A boxing legacy becoming a disciplined community program.

Future Champs is being developed by Glenn Singleton Jr. from family boxing history, community service, and a desire to give young people a safe environment for confidence, fitness, discipline, and mentorship.

Detailed founder and legacy claims will be published only after Glenn reviews and approves the final biography and supporting materials.

Current reality
  • Founder and concept established
  • Food fundraiser selected
  • First pilot being structured
  • Brand and digital systems in development
  • Community and facility relationships being formalized
Future vision
  • Dedicated youth boxing facility
  • Expanded mentoring and community programming
  • Boxing heritage and legacy center
  • Scholarships and regional partnerships
