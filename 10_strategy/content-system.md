# Future Champs Content System — Draft

**Status:** Strategy draft; hold for founder approval  
**Benchmark lenses:** COLLINS for idea-to-world coherence; Punch by Punch for boxing editorial access; MACS Digital Media for production and distribution discipline.

## The job

Future Champs is a youth-and-beginning-fighter boxing media platform. It starts with real access, not a requirement to produce a full season immediately.

The first useful unit is one honest conversation or gym visit.

## The content multiplication rule

One approved recording can become:

1. one full conversation or field episode;
2. three to seven YouTube Shorts;
3. one fighter or coach profile;
4. one quote or lesson card;
5. one short written story for The Corner;
6. one sponsor/community cut when appropriate.

This means the launch can begin with one interview and still feel active, provided every clip is truthful, rights-cleared, captioned, branded, and linked back to the source story.

## Editorial lanes

### Start Here

First-day gym stories, beginner lessons, first fights, first coaches, and what nobody tells you at the beginning.

### In Their Corner

Mentors, parents, coaches, gym owners, teammates, and the people who make progress possible.

### The Work

Training, discipline, setbacks, preparation, recovery, school/work balance, and the daily practice behind improvement.

### Ringside

Event coverage, short interviews, weigh-ins, walk-ins, post-fight reflections, and approved behind-the-scenes moments.

### Future Champs Gives Back

Only defined, approved community or charitable efforts. Do not imply an active campaign without verified details.

## Site structure

The public site should organize the archive by people and stories, not by a generic blog feed:

- **Home** — the current story, the next short, and the one action: watch/follow;
- **Watch** — full conversations and a Shorts-first feed;
- **Fighters** — profiles, starting points, goals, and approved progress stories;
- **Gyms & Corners** — places and mentors that shape fighters;
- **Stories** — written context and searchable archive;
- **About** — Glenn, Future Champs, and the MACS production partnership;
- **Get involved** — guest, coach, sponsor, volunteer, or community lead.

The architecture borrows the principle of a featured-person entrance from Punch by Punch, while owning a different editorial promise: show where people begin and who stands with them.

## Launch sequence

### Pilot 01 — one conversation

Record one approved interview with a beginning fighter, young fighter, coach, or mentor. Cut the strongest moments first for Shorts. The full interview is the anchor, not the prerequisite for publishing.

### Pilot 02 — repeat the format

Repeat the same capture and edit package with a second guest. Compare retention, comments, saves, shares, and qualified guest/community inquiries. Treat platform metrics as evidence, not guaranteed outcomes.

### Pilot 03 — build the archive

Add profiles and story pages only when there is enough approved source material to make them useful.

## MACS Digital Media partnership

Future Champs owns the editorial relationship, host identity, guest permissions, boxing context, and community purpose. MACS Digital Media owns its agreed production, editing, publishing operations, growth experiments, and reusable systems. Each published asset must identify the source show and respect guest/image/music rights.

The relationship should be represented as a production partnership, not as a confusing merger of ownership or mission.

## Content record required before publishing

Every asset needs:

- source recording and guest;
- permission/rights status;
- age/guardian approval where applicable;
- approved name, role, and spelling;
- title and caption;
- platform format;
- content lane;
- status: draft, review, approved, scheduled, published, held;
- owner and next action.

## Anti-slop rules

- No invented records, rankings, affiliations, quotes, outcomes, or testimonials.
- No AI-generated people presented as documentary subjects.
- No generic motivational copy when a real quote or specific lesson exists.
- No overuse of fight-poster typography, flames, chrome, shields, or aggressive silhouettes.
- No publishing before rights and human review are recorded.
- No dashboard complexity exposed to Glenn when a prepared approval queue will do.
