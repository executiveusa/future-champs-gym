# Benchmark and Gauntlet — Future Champs

## Reference roles

### COLLINS — wearecollins.com

Use as the first-class standard for:

- one memorable governing idea;
- strong transformation rather than decoration;
- editorial confidence;
- restraint and hierarchy;
- a coherent world across programs, stories, and calls to action.

Do not copy its layout, language, artwork, or proprietary expression.

### Punch by Punch — punchbypunch.com

Use as the boxing-content structure reference:

- people as the entry point;
- featured fighter-led presentation;
- visual-first access to boxing personalities;
- a site that can hold interviews and clips as an editorial archive.

Future Champs must beat it on the defined territory: youth, beginning fighters, mentorship, and the visible path from starting point to progress.

## Gauntlet gates

Every major output is reviewed independently against both reference lenses and the Future Champs promise:

1. **Idea:** Can a visitor state what Future Champs is and why it is different in one sentence?
2. **Position:** Does the work clearly serve young and beginning fighters rather than boxing audiences in general?
3. **Editorial system:** Can one source recording become a full story plus Shorts without losing context or dignity?
4. **Identity:** Is the system recognizable without relying on the logo, and does it survive black/white, mobile, social, print, and small marks?
5. **Taste:** Does it feel first-class, restrained, documentary, and human?
6. **Anti-slop:** Are generic sports, nonprofit, AI, and template signals removed?
7. **Trust:** Are rights, approvals, current-vs-future claims, and ownership visible and truthful?
8. **Commercial/social value:** Is the next action obvious—watch, follow, submit a guest, partner, sponsor, or support a defined effort?

## Release rule

No logo, website, content template, or publishing automation advances to official delivery until the governing idea is approved, three bounded identity territories are tested, one territory is selected by a human, and the resulting system passes independent design, accessibility, rights, and commercial/desirability review at 9.0 or higher.
