# Anchor Story Brief — The Corner / Pilot 001

## Purpose

Create the first Future Champs story package from one approved video in the canonical Drive archive.

## Editorial promise

Show the person before the result. The audience should leave knowing where this fighter is starting, what they are working on, and who helps them continue.

## Required package

- one clean full-story source video or approved interview destination;
- one 20–60 second vertical Short with a specific hook;
- one alternate Short focused on the person in their corner;
- one 9:16 cover frame;
- captions and transcript;
- one short written profile;
- source and approval record;
- links between the Short, full story, and Future Champs page.

## Editorial constraints

- Use the subject's real words.
- Do not invent a name, age, record, ranking, gym affiliation, or future outcome.
- Do not frame a young person as a spectacle or use pity language.
- Do not publish a minor's likeness without the required guardian/organizational approval.
- Remove platform watermarks only from a clean authorized master; never erase another creator's attribution from a repost.
- Use music only when the license is clear.

## Homepage role

The anchor appears as the first story visitors can watch. Until the package is approved, the site must show a truthful held state rather than a fake episode title or fabricated guest details.
