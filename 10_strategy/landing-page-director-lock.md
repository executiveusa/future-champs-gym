# Landing Page Director Filter — Future Champs

**Status:** Strategy lock draft; implementation remains held until the first source video and founder-approved copy exist.

## Page job

The homepage has one dominant job:

> Make a new visitor watch one real Future Champs story or Short, then take one next step: follow the channel, submit a story/guest, or join the owned audience.

It is not simultaneously a gym brochure, food fundraiser, nonprofit grant page, merch store, and media archive.

## First-glance test

Within five seconds, a visitor must understand:

1. this is Future Champs;
2. it tells boxing stories from the beginning, not only the spotlight;
3. The Corner is the flagship show;
4. there is something real to watch now.

## Landing-page sequence

1. **Hero:** one approved Short or conversation clip; headline: “Every fighter starts somewhere.” Primary action: Watch the latest story.
2. **Proof of difference:** beginning fighters, mentors, gyms, and the work behind progress.
3. **Featured story:** one person, one clear title, one visible source relationship.
4. **Shorts rail:** three to five clips from the same source recording, each linked to its full story where available.
5. **The Corner:** explain the show in one sentence, then show the next episode or held-state honestly.
6. **People and places:** fighters, coaches, gyms, and families only when approved content exists.
7. **MACS partnership:** concise production credit; do not confuse production support with editorial ownership.
8. **One next action:** follow, submit a story, or join updates. Choose one as the launch CTA after observing the first audience.

## Required proof before build claims

- one approved source recording;
- one approved Short or embedded video;
- guest and image permissions;
- guardian review where applicable;
- accurate names, roles, and affiliations;
- visible source/episode relationship;
- no invented audience numbers, sponsor claims, or impact outcomes;
- mobile and desktop composition reviewed separately;
- deployed experience verified before calling it live.

## Copy filter

Use concrete language: “Watch a young fighter talk about their first bout.”

Avoid abstract claims: “We empower the next generation through transformative storytelling.”

The landing page should sound like a first-class editorial platform, not a generic nonprofit template or fight-promotion poster.

## Design implications

The page may borrow proven mechanics—featured people, story archive, Shorts-to-related-video paths, email capture, and story submission—but must develop its own visual behavior from the approved governing idea. Collins is the taste bar, Punch by Punch is the boxing-content bar, and Future Champs wins only when the youth/beginner territory is obvious without explanation.

## Gate

No complete homepage build is approved until the first source asset exists. A polished empty media platform would be a false proof of product value.
