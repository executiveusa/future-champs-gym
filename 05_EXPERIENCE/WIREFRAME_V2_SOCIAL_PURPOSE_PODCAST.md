# Wireframe V2 — Social-Purpose Podcast / Documentary Homepage

**Date:** 2026-10-01  
**Mode:** Brownfield wireframe revision  
**Branch:** `wireframe/home-v2-social-purpose-2026-10-01`

## Project Lock

**Product:** Future Champs  
**Category:** Seattle-based social-purpose boxing podcast and documentary project  
**Flagship show:** The Corner  
**Audience:** Fighters, families, coaches, mentors, gyms, local boxing supporters, community partners, sponsors, and media  
**Primary action:** Watch the latest approved episode/story  
**Secondary action:** Suggest a fighter, coach, mentor, gym, program, or community story  
**Geographic root:** Seattle / Washington  
**Protected asset:** Existing cinematic intro remains unchanged in this slice  
**Content constraint:** No unapproved youth media or invented names, stories, metrics, quotes, or partnerships  
**Design state:** Low fidelity; structure before styling

## Two-second message

A visitor should be able to infer:

1. Future Champs is a Seattle boxing media project.
2. The Corner is its flagship interview/podcast format.
3. The project exists to document fighters at the beginning and the people helping them develop.
4. The main next step is to watch the latest story.

## Governing idea

**Boxing is the setting. People are the story. The Corner is the engine. Seattle is the place.**

## Homepage sequence

1. **Orientation**
   - Seattle, Washington
   - Every fighter starts somewhere.
   - Explicit category line: social-purpose boxing podcast and documentary project
   - Watch the latest
   - The Corner
   - one approved current-episode media slot

2. **The Corner**
   - one featured episode, not a generic three-card row
   - guest / role / gym / factual episode description
   - Watch episode

3. **People in the Corner**
   - fighter
   - coach / mentor
   - relationship is the point, not a roster

4. **From the Gym**
   - training
   - corner work
   - gym details
   - Seattle exterior / environment
   - media rhythm instead of explanatory card grid

5. **Ringside / Field Notes**
   - event video
   - documentary stills
   - crowd / gym / ring details

6. **Lineage**
   - family archive
   - fight posters
   - newspaper clippings
   - Northwest boxing history

7. **Participate**
   - suggest a story
   - partner with Future Champs

## Removed from V1

- equal-weight generic interview cards
- generic three-episode podcast row
- generic gym-profile card as a separate homepage module
- public-facing production-placeholder language such as PROFILE COPY PLACEHOLDER
- repeated card patterns that made every content type feel equally important

## Mobile contract

- Orientation becomes one column.
- Current episode remains above all secondary material.
- Fighter / coach pair stacks vertically.
- From the Gym becomes a horizontal media rail rather than compressed desktop.
- Ringside becomes two-column.
- Archive becomes two-column.
- Participation becomes one column.
- No hover dependency.
- Existing intro reduced-motion behavior remains unchanged.

## Deferred

The current generated knockdown beat in the opening film is not part of this slice. It will be revised separately because the impact does not yet look physically convincing.

## Acceptance test for this wireframe

Without high-fidelity styling, a first-time visitor should be able to answer:

- What is Future Champs?
- Why Seattle?
- What is The Corner?
- Who are the stories about?
- What should I watch first?
- How can I suggest a story?

If those are not clear, styling does not begin.
