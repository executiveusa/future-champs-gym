# Future Champs 30-Day Launch Plan

## Objective

Use one coherent story across Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn to build local recognition, collect consented contacts, validate demand for A Taste of Nawlins, recruit support for The First Five, and establish Glenn Singleton Jr. as a credible founder.

## Geographic focus

Seattle → Shoreline → Lynnwood → Everett → Marysville → Arlington → Mount Vernon.

Do not imply active operations in each city. Use the corridor as the intended community reach and partnership area.

## Audience journeys

### Food customer
Discover menu → join email/preorder list → receive confirmed pop-up date → order → attend or receive delivery → become repeat supporter.

### Parent or guardian
Understand program → see safety and current-stage truth → submit adult interest → receive protected follow-up → decide on enrollment later.

### Sponsor
See specific First Five need → review factual sponsor brief → fund equipment, sessions, food, or operations → receive evidence report.

### Volunteer or mentor
Understand role → submit interest → screening and expectations → approved placement.

### Community partner
See portable program and ghost-kitchen model → offer space, kitchen access, referrals, promotion, equipment, or expertise.

## Content pillars

1. **Founder and legacy** — Glenn’s verified story, values, discipline, and purpose.
2. **The work** — training preparation, curriculum development, safety planning, and operational milestones.
3. **The food** — recipes, process, menu development, pop-up schedule, and Southern hospitality.
4. **The First Five** — why the program starts small, what support funds, and how proof will be measured.
5. **The community build** — partners, volunteers, wish-list items, customer stories, and transparent progress.

## Channel roles

### Instagram
Visual documentary storytelling, food preparation, training moments, short founder clips, menu reveals, stories, and reels.

### Facebook
Local event discovery, neighborhood groups, longer explanations, family audiences, community discussion, and practical updates.

### LinkedIn
Glenn’s founder narrative, partnership requests, progress reports, sponsor credibility, leadership lessons, and social-enterprise development.

## Week 1 — Foundation

- Post 1: Introducing Future Champs — what exists now and what is being built.
- Post 2: Glenn’s founder statement — why youth, boxing, food, and community belong together.
- Post 3: Why start with five — disciplined pilot logic.
- Post 4: A Taste of Nawlins — portable ghost-kitchen and pop-up concept.
- LinkedIn: Create Glenn’s profile with factual headline, founder summary, location, and current-stage language.
- Site: Email signup, event interest, parent interest, sponsor interest, volunteer interest.
- Proof: All accounts use the same approved name, bio, colors, profile mark placeholder, and website link.

## Week 2 — Offer development

- Post 5: Menu development behind the scenes.
- Post 6: What one food purchase is intended to support.
- Post 7: Portable gym concept and equipment categories.
- Post 8: Local partner invitation for kitchens, venues, and boxing resources.
- LinkedIn article: “Why Future Champs is starting small and measuring everything.”
- Site: Public menu section uses Confirmed / Testing / Coming Next statuses.
- Proof: At least ten consented local email or event-interest signups.

## Week 3 — Community participation

- Post 9: Sponsor the First Five equipment framework.
- Post 10: Volunteer and mentor invitation with screening language.
- Post 11: Founder Q&A video.
- Post 12: Food story — origin, taste, hospitality, and Northwest adaptation.
- Facebook: Local community introduction post tailored to Arlington, Marysville, and Everett groups without spam.
- LinkedIn: Direct outreach to local businesses, chambers, gym owners, youth advocates, and kitchen operators.
- Proof: Three qualified partnership conversations and one operational lead.

## Week 4 — Confirmed launch or evidence update

Use one of two tracks.

### Track A — Operational details verified

- Publish confirmed menu, date, address, price, order cap, pickup/delivery terms, allergens, and refund rules.
- Open real preorder or reservation flow.
- Post countdown content.
- Publish where funds go.
- Conduct and document fundraiser.

### Track B — Details still pending

- Do not fabricate urgency.
- Publish a transparent progress update.
- Share recipe testing, kitchen search, equipment planning, and partnership needs.
- Continue list-building and partner outreach.

## Daily publishing rhythm

- Monday: founder/mission
- Tuesday: food/menu
- Wednesday: training/program
- Thursday: community partner or sponsor
- Friday: progress and next milestone
- Weekend: documentary story, live update, or customer/community conversation

Maximum: one primary feed post per day. Stories may support the feed. Do not flood channels with near-duplicate graphics.

## Copy pattern

1. Human truth
2. Specific current action
3. Verified status
4. One invitation

Example:

> Future Champs is beginning with five young people because trust is built through consistent work, not oversized promises. We are currently structuring the pilot, safety process, and startup budget. Join the local update list to follow each verified milestone.

## Email program

### Welcome email
- Thank the person.
- Explain founding stage.
- Ask which updates they want: food, youth program, volunteering, sponsorship.
- Do not automatically enroll them in unrelated lists.

### Weekly update
- One verified milestone.
- One current need.
- One founder or community story.
- One action.

### Raffle and free-food rule

Raffles, sweepstakes, and giveaways require legal and platform review before launch. Until approved, use lawful alternatives such as subscriber-only menu previews, community thank-you drawings with official rules, or first-access invitations. Never imply that a donation is required to enter a promotional drawing without legal review.

## Metrics

- Consent-based subscribers
- Event/preorder interest
- Parent and volunteer leads
- Sponsor and partner leads
- Qualified conversations
- Menu demand by item
- Conversion from social to site
- Repeat engagement
- Verified funds raised
- Verified equipment acquired

Followers alone are not success.

## Approval gates

Human approval required before publishing:

- children or family stories;
- photos or video;
- testimonials;
- health, safety, legal, tax, permit, sponsor, or fiscal-sponsor claims;
- prices, dates, address, allergens, delivery links, raffles, giveaways, and donation language.
