# Tonight V1 Delivery Runbook

**Purpose:** Deliver a private, coherent founder-review version—not a falsely finished public launch.

## 1. Delivery objective

By the end of the meeting, obtain one approved strategic direction and the missing facts required to complete the first public release.

## 2. Required presentation sequence

### A. The problem

Future Champs has strong vision but too many disconnected ideas, visual identities, and future programs. The first job is to create one credible beginning.

### B. The strategy

**Start with five. Feed the movement. Build proof before scale.**

- A Taste of Nawlins creates revenue and audience.
- The First Five creates a bounded program.
- Future Champs publishes evidence and earns expansion.

### C. The public experience

Show the founder:

1. Founding-stage hero
2. Menu and portable pop-up model
3. How food supports the pilot
4. First Five explanation
5. Founder story
6. Current reality vs future vision
7. Ways to participate
8. Transparent milestones

### D. The approval decisions

Ask Glenn to approve, correct, or reject each item.

## 7. Design-review questions

Do not ask only whether he likes the site.

Ask:

1. What does this organization do?
2. What is happening first?
3. What should a visitor do?
4. What feels most like you?
5. What feels false or exaggerated?
6. What is missing?
7. What should never appear?
8. Which part would you proudly show another person tonight?
9. Which part would confuse a parent?
10. What should somebody remember tomorrow?

## 8. Collins-level review gate

The direction passes founder review when:

- one governing idea is visible;
- the organization feels recognizable rather than generic;
- food and boxing are connected by strategy, not decoration;
- the founder appears credible and human;
- the visual system can extend to social, flyers, menu, apparel, and future facility materials;
- no category cliché controls the experience;
- visitors can understand the offer immediately.

## 9. Krug usability gate

The private V1 fails when Glenn repeatedly asks:

- Where is the menu?
- What do I click?
- What does this mean?
- Is the gym already open?
- Is this a real order?
- Where does the money go?
- What is The First Five?
- How do I change this?

## 10. Post-meeting outputs

## 11. Public-launch blockers

Do not publish until the following are resolved:

```text
[ ] Legal operating location
[ ] Kitchen and permit status
[ ] Menu and prices
[ ] Payment and fulfillment
[ ] Refund and allergen language
[ ] Fiscal-sponsor/donation language
[ ] Youth safety and privacy
[ ] Founder factual approval
[ ] Working form persistence
[ ] Mobile QA
[ ] Independent review
[ ] Rollback
```

## 12. Meeting close

End with one decision:

> Approve this strategic direction as the basis for the first public Future Champs launch, subject to the factual corrections recorded tonight.

Do not seek approval for a final logo, permanent gym, large program, or full organizational architecture tonight.
