### Day 3 — Operations lock

Update the site with real content, forms, menu status, milestones, and founder story.

### Day 5 — Identity and campaign

Create the black-and-white vector direction, first flyer family, and social profile package.

### Day 6 — Verification

Test mobile, forms, privacy, accessibility, copy accuracy, and rollback.

### Day 7 — Controlled release

Launch the menu-interest funnel or fundraiser only if all release gates pass.
