02 / The people in the corner

MEET THE COACHES.

The careers behind the gyms, and the people who train the next generation.

01 / Arlington, Washington

Bret Summers

Coach · Summers Boxing Club

Bret Summers began boxing at age eight, became a national amateur champion in 1976, and finished his professional career with a 28–3–1 record, according to the Snohomish County Sports Commission. He now coaches amateur and professional boxers in Arlington.

02 / Burlington, Washington

Sylvester “Doc” Barron

Founder and coach · Barron's Boxing

Sylvester “Doc” Barron is a U.S. Marine Corps veteran and former professional heavyweight boxer. After his fighting career, he founded Barron’s Boxing in Burlington, where he coaches beginners and experienced athletes.

03 / Seattle, Washington

Manuel “Manny” Dunham

Founder and coach · Nomad Boxing Club

Manuel “Manny” Dunham founded Nomad Boxing Club in 2019. According to Nomad’s own biography, he spent 27 years boxing and brings that experience into coaching people with a wide range of fitness and competition goals.
